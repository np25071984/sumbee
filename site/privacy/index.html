Sumbee Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

In short: Sumbee collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. The little it remembers stays on your device.

Sumbee is a math practice app for children aged 5–12, made by Nikolai Poriadin. This policy explains what the app does with information, for parents, guardians and anyone else using it.

What the app stores

To save a few taps each day, Sumbee remembers these on the device:

  • the name typed on the Setup screen, if any (it is optional, and a first name or nickname is enough);
  • the last-used settings: how big the numbers go, which operations to practise and how many cards.

Scores, times and answers are not stored. Each session is forgotten when it ends.

What the app collects or shares

Nothing. Sumbee has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code. It does not ask for any permissions such as camera, microphone, location or contacts.

The stored name and settings are also excluded from Android backup and device transfer, so they are not copied to a cloud account.

Children's privacy

Sumbee is made for children and is designed so that no personal information leaves the device. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to review, share, sell or delete.

Deleting the stored data

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings (Apps → Sumbee → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the app. To remove only the name, clear it on the Setup screen and tap Start.

Google Play

If you install Sumbee from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download under its own privacy policy. The developer receives no personal information from it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that would collect information would also require a new app version.
