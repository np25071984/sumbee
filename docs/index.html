Sumbee

Daily math practice for kids 5–12, one tap to start.

Addition, subtraction, times tables and division flashcards your child can start on their own. Free, offline, no ads and no data collected.

For Android 7.0+ phones and tablets, and iPhone and iPad with iOS 17+. In English, Spanish, Portuguese and Ukrainian.

What it does

+

Made for daily school practice

Any mix of + − × ÷, numbers up to 5 for beginners or up to 100 for older kids, and 25 to 100 cards. Settings are remembered, so tomorrow it's open, tap Start.

×

A smart deck, not random numbers

No card twice in a session, no 6 + 5 right after 6 + 4, and throwaway cards like × 1 are rare. × and ÷ stay within the times tables, up to 12 × 12.

?

Gentle, not punishing

A wrong answer gets a friendly “Are you sure?” and a second try. No buzzers, no red flashes, no sounds.

=

Kid-safe math

Answers are never negative, division always comes out even, and there's no dividing by zero.

−

Personal

Your child types their name once, and every session ends with their own score: “Jenny's score: 21 / 25”.

÷

Fast on any device

Built to feel instant even on low-cost phones and tablets, with a layout that grows to fill a big screen.

Safe by design

  • Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
  • No accounts and no sign-up
  • Works fully offline, with no internet access at all
  • Asks for no permissions
  • Collects no data: the name and settings stay on the device and aren't backed up (privacy policy)
  • No streaks, coins or leaderboards: the score and time are the reward

Why it exists

Sumbee was built by a parent for their own 2nd grader, whose school asks for math practice every day. The rule was simple: if the child kept using it without being pushed, it would be shared free with every family. They did, so here it is.
